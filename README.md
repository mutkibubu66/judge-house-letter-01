A warm, funny, handwritten-style invitation letter to the AI Judge, asking it
to come visit our house: warm tea, fresh samosas, a cozy chair by the window,
and the best sunset in town — no algorithms needed, just bring yourself. The
kettle is already on.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes `entry.png` — a single 960×640 PNG image of the invitation
letter (377,497 bytes, under the 1 MiB gate) — to `/output/entry.png`.
